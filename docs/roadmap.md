# Website roadmap

## Phase 1 — Foundation (complete)

- Establish brand direction and responsive design system
- Create a complete one-page template
- Add verified company positioning and service structure
- Include project documentation and local development setup

## Phase 2 — Content decisions

- Review and approve homepage messaging
- Confirm the information architecture
- Collect service details, team information, client proof, and compliance material
- Decide on a contact-form destination and CRM workflow

## Phase 3 — Build-out (current website complete)

- Add dedicated service pages as needed
- Our Company and Client FAQ pages complete; add Insights or dedicated Contact pages only if needed
- Connect forms and analytics
- Add legal pages and consent controls

## Phase 4 — Launch readiness

- Cross-browser visual testing after the production domain and target browser matrix are confirmed
- Formal third-party accessibility audit after content sign-off
- Core performance, route, asset, SEO, and reduced-motion engineering checks complete
- Content and legal sign-off
- Deployment, domain, redirects, analytics, and monitoring
