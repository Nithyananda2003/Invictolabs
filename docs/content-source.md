## Deliberate content guardrails

The template does not invent:

- Customer names or logos
- Testimonials
- Completion-time guarantees
- Accuracy, savings, or volume statistics
- Security or compliance certifications
- Specific technology integrations

These can be added after Invicto supplies approved evidence and wording.
